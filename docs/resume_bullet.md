# Resume bullet (measured results only)

_Rendered from `resume_bullet.tmpl.md`; every number is a ledger value._

- Built a pre-registered mineral prospectivity study on USGS NURE stream-sediment geochemistry and MRDS gold producers over Nevada (5707 grid cells, 13771 samples, 2850 producer sites): compositional log-ratio features, two models under random and spatial block cross-validation with repeated allocations, and a frozen decision rule. Showed that random k-fold overstated the boosted model's AUC (0.638 versus 0.571 with 100 km blocks chosen from the target's autocorrelation range) and that the laboratory was learnable (leave-one-family-out AUC 0.488); every reported number is traceable from extract to script to ledger to report through a verification harness with a clean re-execution.
